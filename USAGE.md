# Usage and Consent Policy

This software, PrecisionDrv, is distributed as an **open-source research and development tool**.  
It is intended only for **lawful, ethical, and consensual uses**.

## 1. Acceptable use
PrecisionDrv may be used for:
- Accessibility and assistive technologies;
- Educational, academic, or research purposes;
- Controlled industrial or automation environments (e.g. SCADA);
- Legitimate software customization and input device integration.

Any use must be transparent, with explicit consent from the user of the system where the driver is installed.

## 2. Prohibited use
It is strictly forbidden to use this software for:
- Unauthorized data collection (e.g., keylogging, spying, surveillance);
- Circumventing anti-cheat or DRM systems;
- Controlling computers without the owner’s consent;
- Any commercial deployment without appropriate code signing and legal compliance;
- Any malicious, fraudulent, or privacy-invasive purpose.

Violation of these conditions may result in the revocation of digital certificates and potential legal consequences under applicable laws.

## 3. Consent and installation
By installing or using this driver, the user explicitly agrees that:
- They understand the driver operates in kernel mode and intercepts input events;
- They are responsible for the configuration and consequences of its use;
- Installation is performed knowingly, with administrative privileges, and only on systems they own or are authorized to modify.
